Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  By David Lloyd Ltd

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how By David Lloyd collects, uses and safeguards your information when you use our website or get in touch with us.

Who we are

By David Lloyd Ltd is the data controller responsible for your personal data. We operate bydavidlloyd.com and related sub-domains. For any privacy-related questions, contact us at info@bydavidlloyd.com.

What data we collect

We may collect the following when you use our website or submit an enquiry:

  • Your name and contact details (email, phone number)
  • Information about your property preferences
  • Technical data such as IP address and browser type
  • Usage data such as pages visited and time on site

How we use your data

We use your data to respond to your enquiries, provide information about our properties, send you updates (with your consent), and improve our website. We will never sell your personal data to third parties.

Legal basis

We process your data under UK GDPR on the following bases: your consent, the performance of a contract, our legitimate interests, or a legal obligation.

Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies to keep our site running, and analytics cookies to understand how visitors use the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data. You may also object to processing or request data portability. To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@bydavidlloyd.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Data retention & security

We retain your data only as long as necessary — typically up to 2 years for enquiry data. We use HTTPS encryption and appropriate security measures to protect your information.

Complaints

If you have concerns, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Questions about this policy?

We're happy to help. Get in touch and we'll respond within one working day.
